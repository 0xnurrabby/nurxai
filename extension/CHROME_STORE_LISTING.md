NurAi is an AI reply assistant for X/Twitter that helps you write sharper, more natural replies without sounding like a bot.

Open a reply box on X and NurAi gives you 4 short, contextual reply options in seconds. Pick the one that fits, paste it manually, edit if you want, then post when you are ready.

Built for creators, marketers, founders, crypto/Web3 communities, and anyone who wants to stay active on X without writing every reply from scratch.

WHY NURAI FEELS DIFFERENT

Most AI reply tools are easy to spot. They over-explain, use the same phrases, and sound too polished.

NurAi is tuned for replies that feel closer to how people actually write on X:

* short, contextual suggestions
* no emoji-heavy AI tone
* avoids common AI openers like "Great point" and "Absolutely"
* uses natural phrasing, casual rhythm, and human imperfections
* understands the post before writing
* keeps you in control with manual paste only

WHAT NURAI CAN DO

* Generate 4 unique reply suggestions for a tweet
* Understand tweet text, quoted posts, links, and visible context
* Read images, charts, memes, and screenshots on Pro and Premium
* Match your tone with 7 styles: Default, Funny, Short, Productive, Professional, Supportive, and Contrarian
* Let you add a custom style note so replies sound more like you
* Save project context for topics like Base, Solana, your startup, a token, a product, or a community
* Regenerate a fresh set of replies when the first set is not the right angle
* Show dashboard notifications and global chat updates from the extension panel
* Paste only when you click Use. NurAi never auto-posts.

HOW IT WORKS

1. Install the NurAi Chrome extension.
2. Create an account at https://nurxai.xyz.
3. Start the free trial or choose a plan.
4. Open X.com and click Reply on any post.
5. NurAi appears beside the reply box with 4 suggestions.
6. Click Use to paste a reply.
7. Edit it if you want, then post it yourself.

PRIVACY

NurAi only works when you open a reply composer and use the extension.

NurAi does not collect:

* your X/Twitter password
* your DMs or private messages
* your full browsing history
* tweets you are not replying to
* wallet custody information

NurAi uses account email for login, selected tweet context for reply generation, and usage counts for plan limits.
